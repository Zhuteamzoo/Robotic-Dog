# V2

## Motor Holder

There are a lot of modifications I made to the motor holder, technically I'm at V4 for the motor holer, but I will summarize all the changes I made in V2,V3,V4 in this one document. Firstly I added some holes on the bottom of the motor carrier so that the wires of the motor could fit through and be able to connect itself to the moteus r4.11. Although the holes weren't properly placed so the motor wires couldn't actually fit through. So I added another hole in V3, but that hole still was in the wrong place. For testing purposes, I heat gunned and connected the 2 holes togheter so that the wires could fit. I heat gunned so that I could test out my motor for the first time and the readings for the position kept reading as 0. This was because i didn't know that the magnet actually had to spin with the motor and i just put it on a little pouch at the back of the motor. V4 is probably the last one unless I find a new erroe (I probably will lol), in this version I definitely made the most changes out of all the previous versions. I connected the 2 holes togheter so that I could actually finally fit the wires through. AFter that I fixed the magnet issue and I added a hole on the bottom of the mtoor holder with some tolerance between it and the magnet holder so that my moteus r4.11's encoder could read the magnets magnetic field and know where my motor was. Also for this version I added a little bubble like hole on the bottom of the motor holder so that the port on my moteus wouldn't hit the motor holder and restrict the encoder to get close enough to the magnet to read it properly. Since when the encoder is to far from the magnet it won't properly read.
