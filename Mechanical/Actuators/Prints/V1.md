## Mistake 1

The little half circles on the Moteus Holder for the screws broke since they were too weak.


<img width="1536" height="2048" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc15f5fb-db8d-4596-ba1a-e161f1329118" />


I will try to reinforce that part so that it doesn't snap off so easy again since this part is pretty important. If the half circle things break then the screws will come loose and the moteus will exposed leading to damage. If the moteus gets damaged I will be losing a lot of money.

## Mistake 2

The printing settings on my Bambu Lab P1S were a bit off and the under side of the motor carrier was printed out weird. I think that this was caused since this is where the supports were connected to the Motor Holder so the top Z distance was a bit too high probably. I set a high top Z distance to have easily removable supports, but this also made it print out werid and all rough. 


<img width="1536" height="2048" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bdcf7928-b30e-4a3f-adbc-eea01953cabe" />


I will try to find the optimal top Z distance such that the supports will be easy to remove while still leaving the base of the motor holder nice and smooth. I could also print the supports out of PLA and main body out of PETG since PETG and PLA don't bond well and the supports will be very easy to remove. Altough, I've never tried to do this trick before and it might cause some problems of its own.
